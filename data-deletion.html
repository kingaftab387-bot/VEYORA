User Data Deletion

Last updated: September 14, 2026

You can request deletion of your VEYORA account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Email mraftab00000@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Include your VEYORA account email or user ID and write “Data Deletion Request” in the subject.

What may be deleted

We will delete or anonymize your profile, login-linked account record, profile photo, matching preferences, and other personal information associated with your account, subject to lawful retention requirements.

Processing

We will review the request, may ask for reasonable verification, and process it within a reasonable period. Some records may be retained temporarily for fraud prevention, safety investigations, backups, or legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymized.
